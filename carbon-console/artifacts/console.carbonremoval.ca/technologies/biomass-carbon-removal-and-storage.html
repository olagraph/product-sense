Biomass carbon removal and storage

Plant life, like dead trees or leftover crop waste after a harvest, has a secret superpower: they absorb CO₂ over their lifetime. BiCRS harnesses this natural process by collecting this carbon-rich biomass and either burying it directly underground or processing it into stable forms that lock carbon away permanently. This sustainable biomass, whether from a farmer's field or part of forest fire management plans, pauses the natural decomposition process that would normally release stored carbon back to the atmosphere.

BiCRS is inclusive of multiple sub-technologies, including biochar, biomass burial, or bio-oil injection.

BiCRS transforms agricultural and forestry waste into permanent carbon storage solutions that generate revenue for farmers and forest managers while preventing organic materials from naturally releasing their stored carbon back to the atmosphere, creating new markets for biomass specifically designed for climate impact rather than traditional uses.
