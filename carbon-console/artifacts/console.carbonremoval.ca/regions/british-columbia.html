Carbon removal policy environment

  • British Columbia currently operates a provincial industrial carbon pricing system known as the B.C. Output-Based Pricing System (BC OBPS).

  • CO2 storage regulations in place
    Yes
  • Offsets as compliance option under carbon pricing system
    Yes

  • BC allows for the use of permanent carbon removal technologies through its CCS Protocol, which is an open-ended approach that includes multiple technologies like direct air capture, BECCS, and storage via carbon mineralization.

  • Provincial funding support
    No
  • Geologic storage potential
    1B – 4B tonnes
