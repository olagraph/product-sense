Carbon removal policy environment

  • Alberta currently operates a provincial industrial carbon pricing system known as the Technology Innovation and Emissions Reduction (TIER).

  • CO2 storage regulations in place
    Yes
  • Offsets as compliance option under carbon pricing system
    Yes
  • Protocol availability for carbon removal offsets
    Yes

  • The Alberta Carbon Capture Incentive Program (ACCIP) is intended to provide grant support of up to 12% for qualifying capital expenditures toward DAC and BECCS projects. It has been designed to be stackable with federal CCUS ITC, resulting in combined 72% ITC for eligible projects.
