Carbon removal policy environment

  • Quebec currently operates a provincial cap-and-trade industrial carbon pricing system known as the Carbon Market.

  • As of February 2026, legislation that would regulate carbon storage has been introduced in Quebec under Bill 17. The bill aims to regulate the exploration and application of underground reservoirs, including the use of the pore space for carbon storage.

  • Offsets as compliance option under carbon pricing system
    Yes

  • Existing protocols recognize offsets generated through afforestation and reforestation. However, there are no protocols that recognize durable carbon removal.

  • Provincial funding support
    No
